NMR Tutor Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-22

NMR Tutor is designed to work without an account or developer-operated online service. The app does not collect data or track you.

Data collection and tracking

The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or developer-operated network service. NMR Tutor does not send app usage or imported scientific data to the developer.

Imported files and saved work

NMR datasets are opened and processed on your device. Files and workspaces remain in locations you control and leave the app only when you explicitly export, copy, or share them.

Graph audio

Graph audio is generated from chart data for playback or export. NMR Tutor does not use the microphone to record audio.

Support correspondence

If you email support, you choose what information to provide. The developer uses it only to respond to and manage the support request and retains it only as long as reasonably needed for that purpose. Do not send confidential or personally identifiable scientific datasets unless necessary and appropriate.

This website

This static website adds no analytics, advertising, contact forms, or tracking cookies. GitHub hosts the site and may process ordinary technical request information under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Questions or requests can be sent to utchemnmr@gmail.com.
