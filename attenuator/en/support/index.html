Attenuator Support

Use these checks for common questions, contact the developer privately, or report a reproducible issue publicly.

Power or pulse results

Check the selected Vpp or Vrms convention, impedance, dB/dBm values, unit prefix, and NMR reference pulse and power before comparing results.

Barometer

Barometer requires a device with a pressure sensor and may show Apple’s Motion permission dialog. The reading is processed on-device and is not tracked or sent to the developer.

Magnetic field

Field requires a device magnetometer and may show Apple’s Location permission dialog because the app reads heading sensor data. Readings stay on-device. Keep the phone away from extremely strong magnetic fields.
