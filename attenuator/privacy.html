Attenuator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-22

Attenuator does not collect data or track you. Calculations, settings, and sensor readings remain on your device.

Data collection and tracking

Attenuator has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated online service. The developer does not collect data from the app.

Barometer and Motion permission

The Barometer feature uses the device pressure sensor and may trigger Apple’s Motion permission dialog. Pressure readings are processed on-device and are not collected, stored by the developer, or transmitted.

Magnetic field and Location permission

The Field feature uses heading data from the device magnetometer and may trigger Apple’s Location permission dialog. Attenuator uses this access only to display the local magnetic-field reading. The app does not track, collect, store, or transmit your location or sensor reading.

Settings

Calculator values, units, and preferences are stored locally on the device using Apple’s standard settings storage.

Support correspondence

If you email support, you choose what information to provide. It is used only to address the support request and retained only as long as reasonably needed for that purpose.

This website

This static website adds no owner-controlled analytics, advertising, forms, or tracking cookies. GitHub hosts the site and may process ordinary technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. Questions can be sent to utchemnmr@gmail.com.

The English privacy policy is authoritative. Translations are provided for convenience.
